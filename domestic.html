The jobs we are the right firm for, and the ones we are not

Said plainly, because it saves everybody a phone call.

⭐ A good fit

  • A full EICR with a written report
  • A new build house, start to finish
  • A full or partial rewire
  • A consumer unit replacement
  • A planned project with a date on it

⚠ Not what we do

  • Single small jobs such as changing one light fitting or one socket
  • Fault finding call-outs
  • Emergency call-outs

🔴 We are an installation and testing contractor rather than a call-out service. If you need somebody out today for one fitting, another electrician will serve you better and we would rather say so now.
