Common questions

Do I need a battery?

Not always. It depends on when you use electricity. If the house is empty during the day, a battery usually makes the panels far more useful.

Can my existing board take it?

We check before you order anything. If it needs work, you are told up front, not on install day.

Do you do the electrics as well as the panels?

Yes. One NICEIC approved contractor for the panels, the inverter and the board.
