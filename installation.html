Common questions

Can we stay in the house during a rewire?

Often, yes. It depends on the size of the house and the scope, which is why we survey first and tell you before the work is booked.

Do you do small one-off jobs?

We are set up for planned installation and testing work rather than single small jobs or same-day visits. If you need one socket moved today, another electrician will serve you better.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. New installation work is certified on completion by a NICEIC approved contractor.
