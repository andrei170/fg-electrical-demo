What the law now requires in Northern Ireland

The Electrical Safety Standards for Private Tenancies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024.

  • Every private rented property needs its fixed electrical installation inspected and tested by a qualified electrician at least every five years
  • New tenancies from 1 April 2025, and existing tenancies by 1 December 2025
  • The report must go to new tenants before they move in, to existing tenants within 28 days of the test, and to the council within 7 days if asked
  • Councils can fine landlords up to £5,000 for not complying

⚠ Summarised from published guidance (NAPIT, CAAV and the Department for Communities). Check the regulations themselves for your own situation.
