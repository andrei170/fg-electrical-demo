What the report codes mean

So you know what has to be paid for, and what can wait. More on EICRs, codes and certificates →

C1: danger present

Immediate risk. Made safe straight away.

C2: potentially dangerous

Must be put right for the installation to be satisfactory.

C3: improvement recommended

Not a failure. Something to plan for when convenient.

FI: further investigation

Something we could not fully assess on the day, and need to look at further.

Common questions

How often does a commercial building need testing?

It depends on the use. IET Guidance Note 3 recommends intervals by building type, typically five years for shops and offices and shorter for industrial premises. We will tell you which applies to yours.

Can you test without closing us?

In most cases, yes. We programme the work around your opening hours, early, late or across several visits.

Do you work for main contractors?

Yes. We take on the electrical package on fit-outs and refurbishments for contractors and developers.
